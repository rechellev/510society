03How it works

How partnering works

Pick an event, pick a package, and we handle the rest. Every activation is co-branded and built around your goals, so your product becomes part of the day, not a booth in the corner.

The processThree steps from first call to final numbers

1

Pick your moment

Choose one event, a whole season, or a cultural weekend from the 2027 calendar.

2

We build you in

We work your brand into the day itself: the bar, the mats, the goodie bags, the photo wall.

3

You get the recap

Photos, content, reach, and guest numbers, delivered within two weeks.
