Our story

One court and a group chat that got out of hand

510 began in 2025 with a simple idea: the Bay needed a place to work out and hang out at the same time. The first Tennis & Tequila sold out. Then it sold out again.

Today we run tennis, pickleball, Pilates, and yoga socials across the East Bay.

Mackenzie Majors

“One year ago, I took a chance on an idea that wellness and libations could exist in the same space. That you could find community over a tennis class, a Pilates workout, or a tequila shot. A year later, that idea has become so much bigger than I ever imagined.”
